Ackamma Pallivathukkal – Biography & Family History

Pallivathukkal Family, Ramapuram • Adityapuram Bungalow, Kanjirappally • (1892 – 1967)

Ackamma, the tenth child of fifth-generation patriarch KunjuVarkey Kuruvila (and belonging to the sixth generation of the Kallarackal / Anathanam family), was born on August 28, 1892.

Ackamma was married to Advocate George Thomas, B.A., B.L., hailing from the eminent Pallivathukkal family in Ramapuram. To them were born three children belonging to the seventh generation: Raytom (George Thomas, I.A.S.), Babu (Vavachan), and Pennamma.

While residing at the family estate residence, Adityapuram Bungalow in Kanjirappally, Ackamma was called to eternal rest on May 2, 1967. Her husband, Shri George Thomas B.A., B.L., had passed away earlier on December 27, 1962.

The Children of Ackamma & George Thomas (7th Generation)

1. George Thomas, I.A.S. (Raytom)

Ackamma's eldest child, George Thomas, married Kunjamma of the prominent Panikulam family from Thrissur. An esteemed officer of the Indian Administrative Service (I.A.S.), he rendered distinguished service as the District Collector of Kottayam. Subsequently, he served the state as Acting Chief Secretary to the Government of Kerala prior to his retirement. Following his civil service career, he resides in Thiruvananthapuram.

2. Babu (Vavachan)

Ackamma's second child, Vavachan (Babu), married Marykutty of the respected Perumpallichira family in Kothamangalam. He resides with his family in Kanjirappally.

3. Pennamma

Ackamma's third child and daughter, Pennamma, was married to K. P. Joseph of the notable Konnackal family in Kuravilangad. Shri K. P. Joseph served with distinction as an Accountant General (A.G.) under the Central Government of India. They reside in Madras (Chennai).
