Thresiamma & Dr. P. C. Vargheese – Biography & Family History

Puthenpurayil Family, Kavalam • Porunnukattu, Ponkunnam • (b. 1898)

Thresiamma, the twelfth child of fifth-generation patriarch KunjuVarkey Kuruvila (and belonging to the sixth generation of the Kallarackal / Anathanam family), was born on December 15, 1898.

Thresiamma was given in marriage to Dr. P. C. Vargheese (Varghese), L.M.S., Civil Surgeon, son of Chacko and Elimma of the venerable Puthenpurayil (Puthenpura) family in Kavalam, Changanacherry. To this couple were born five children belonging to the seventh generation: Jacob, Eliamma, Kuruvila, Thressi, and Joseph.

Dr. P. C. Vargheese, a distinguished medical officer and Civil Surgeon, resided with his family at their estate property, Porunnukattu, near Ponkunnam.

The Children of Thresiamma & Dr. P. C. Vargheese (7th Generation)

1. Jacob Kavalam

The eldest child, Jacob, was born on October 1, 1927. A distinguished scientist and electronic engineer, he qualified with B.Sc., Dip. Teach, and A.M.I.E.R.E. (Associate Member of the Institution of Electronic and Radio Engineers). He served in an executive capacity as Assistant Director at the Electronics and Radar Development Establishment (L.R.D.E.) in Bangalore. He married Sally, B.A., daughter of T. J. Mathew of the Thelliyil family. Sally passed away on September 16, 1965. Jacob resides with his children in Bangalore.

2. Eliamma

The second child, Eliamma, was born on March 5, 1929. She was married to P. D. George, B.A., of the Pallikkaparambil family in Mutholapuram, who served with high merit in the state police as Deputy Superintendent of Police (Dy. S.P.). Eliamma passed away on January 11, 1962. P. D. George resides with his children in Ernakulam.

3. Kuruvila Kavalam, B.Sc. (Eng.)

The third child, Kuruvila (twin with Thressi), was born on December 24, 1930. An engineer by profession graduating with B.Sc. (Eng.), he married Reethamma (Rita) of the well-known Vellanikkaran family from Ernakulam. They reside with their family in Thiruvananthapuram.

4. Thressi, B.A.

The fourth child, Thressi (twin with Kuruvila), was born on December 24, 1930. Having earned her B.A. degree, she was married to P. V. Mathai, B.Sc. (Eng.), an engineer hailing from the Payyappilly family in Moozhikkulam. They reside with their family in Ranchi (Jharkhand).

5. Dr. Joseph Varghese, B.Sc., M.B.B.S.

The fifth child, Dr. Joseph Varghese, was born on August 18, 1935. He completed his graduation with B.Sc. and medical qualification M.B.B.S., joining the medical profession. He married Mariyamma of the distinguished Karimbanal family in Kanjirappally. Dr. Joseph serves as a doctor on the medical staff of Caritas Hospital (Thellakom, Kottayam) and resides with his family on the hospital grounds.
