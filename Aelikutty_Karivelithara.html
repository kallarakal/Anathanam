Family History & Biography – Aelikutty & Chackochan (Karivelithara)

14th Child of Anathanam KunjuVarkey Kuruvila • Sixth Generation Kallarackal Anathanam • Mampuzhakary

Aelikutty (ഏലിക്കുട്ടി), the fourteenth child of Anathanam KunjuVarkey Kuruvila, was married to Chackochan (ചാക്കോച്ചൻ) of the prominent Karivelithara (കരിവേലിത്തറ) family in Mampuzhakary (മാമ്പിഴക്കരി / Mampizhakari).

To this union were born seven children belonging to the Seventh Generation: Kama, Thankamma, Kuruvachan, Marymma, Omana, Leelamma, and Cisy.

1. Kama (കാമ)

The eldest child, Kama, was married to Anthonikutty of the well-known Choolackal (ചൂളക്കൽ) family in Ernakulam. They reside with their family in Ernakulam.

2. Thankamma (തങ്കമ്മ)

The second child, Thankamma, was married to Mathachan of the distinguished Eerathottil (ഈററത്തോട്ടിൽ) family from Kalaketty. They reside with their family in Ernakulam.

3. Kuruvachan (കുറുവച്ചൻ / കുവച്ചൻ)

The third child and only son, Kuruvachan (Kuvachan), devoted himself to overseeing and managing the ancestral landed estates, agricultural properties, and family affairs. He resides at Karivelithara together with his parents.

4. The Physician Sisters: Dr. Marymma, Dr. Omana & Dr. Leelamma

In an extraordinary academic and professional distinction for the family, three daughters—Mrs. Dr. Marymma, Miss Dr. Omana, and Miss Dr. Leelamma—each earned their Doctor of Medicine degrees in Italy. Following their medical graduation in Italy, they completed advanced clinical and professional hospital training in England, and have been dedicatedly serving in the medical profession.

5. Miss Cisy, B.A. (Miss. സിസി)

The youngest daughter, Miss Cisy, graduated with her Bachelor of Arts (B.A.) degree and is actively pursuing advanced higher education.
