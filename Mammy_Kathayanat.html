5-ാം തലമുറക്കാരനായ കുഞ്ഞുവർക്കി കുരുവിളയുടെ ഒൻപതാമത്തെ സന്താനമായ മാമ്മി (മറിയ) 1890 ഏപ്രിൽ 4-ാം തീയതി ജനിച്ചു. മാമ്മിയെ തമ്പലക്കാട്ടു കുന്നുംപുറത്തു കുടുംബ ശാഖയായ കതയനാട്ടു് മത്തായി തോമസ് വിവാഹം ചെയ്തു. ഇദ്ദേഹം മുൻ തിരുവിതാംകൂർ പ്രജാസഭയിലെ ഒരു അംഗമായിരുന്നു. ഇവർക്കു് ഏലിക്കുട്ടി, മത്തായിച്ചൻ, കുരുവിള, ത്രേസ്യാമ്മ, മേരി, മാത്യ, തോമസ്, ജോസഫ്, സെബാസ്റ്റിയൻ, അന്നമ്മ, ക്ലാരമ്മ, റോസക്കുട്ടി എന്നിങ്ങനെ 12 സന്താനങ്ങൾ ഉണ്ടായി.

Mammy (Mariya) Kathayanat – Biography & Family History

Kunnumpuram Branch, Kathayanat Family, Thambalakkad • (1890 – 1936)

Mammy (Mariya), the ninth child of the fifth-generation patriarch KunjuVarkey Kuruvila (and belonging to the sixth generation of the Kallarackal / Anathanam family), was born on April 4, 1890.

Mammy was given in marriage to Mathai Thomas (Thommachan) Kathayanat, belonging to the prominent Kathayanat branch of the distinguished Kunnumpuram family in Thambalakkad. Shri Mathai Thomas was a respected civic leader who served as an elected Member of the historic Travancore Sree Moolam Praja Sabha (the Popular Assembly of Travancore).

To this venerable couple were born 12 children belonging to the seventh generation: Elikutty, Mathaichan, Kuruvila, Thresiamma, Mary (Chinnamma), Mathew (Kuttiyachan), Thomas, Joseph, Sebastian, Annamma, Claramma, and Rosakutty.

Their father, Thommachan, continues to reside at the Kathayanat ancestral home together with his son. His devoted wife, Mammy, passed away into eternal glory on March 2, 1936.

The 12 Children of Mammy & Mathai Thomas (7th Generation)

1. Elikutty

The eldest daughter, Elikutty, was married in 1919 to Thomas of the respected Kottavathukkal family in Palai. They were blessed with two children. Their son, Air Commodore K. T. Abraham, had a distinguished military career in the Indian Air Force, serving as the Western Area Air Defence Chief, and resides in Ambala.

2. Mathaichan

Mammy's second child, Mathaichan, passed away in infancy.

3. Kuruvila (Kuruvachan), B.A.

The third child, Kuruvila (Kuruvachan), graduated with a Bachelor of Arts degree and subsequently felt called to religious consecrated life, joining the renowned Jesuit Mission (Society of Jesus / S.J.). However, before completing his priestly theological studies, he was called to eternal rest on October 11, 1954.

4. Thresiamma, B.A. (Chachikunju)

The fourth child, Thresiamma B.A. (Chachikunju), was an accomplished educator who rendered dedicated service as a high school teacher at St. Anne's High School, Kottayam. She was married to Advocate Mathew Maniangadan. They were blessed with six children. Thresiamma passed away on November 9, 1958.

5. Chinnamma / Sr. Mary Francis, B.A., B.T.

The fifth child, Chinnamma (Mary), joined the Adoration Congregation (Sisters of the Adoration of the Blessed Sacrament – S.A.B.S.), taking the religious name Sr. Mary Francis, B.A., B.T. She served for many years as a dedicated teacher in a high school administered by the congregation. Following retirement, she leads a quiet and prayerful retired life at the Vazhappally Convent.

6. Prof. K. T. Mathai, M.A. (Kuttiyachan)

The sixth child, Kuttiyachan (K. T. Mathai M.A.), married in 1942 to Mariyamma, daughter of P. V. Poulose, Superintendent in the Accounts Office, Trivandrum. A distinguished scholar of English literature, he served as a Lecturer in English at St. Xavier's College, Palayamkottai, and St. Berchmans (S.B.) College, Changanacherry. Later, he joined the faculty of University College, Trivandrum, and currently serves as Professor of English at Government College, Thalassery.

7. Adv. K. T. Thomas, B.A., B.L.

The seventh child, K. T. Thomas B.A., B.L., entered the legal profession and practices actively as an Advocate residing in Ponkunnam. He is blessed with five children:

  • Mariyamma
  • Cicily (Chechamma)
  • Maria Goretti (fondly known to her husband at home and in America as Mariyamma)
  • Rani (Ranima)
  • Thomas

8. Er. K. T. Joseph, B.Sc. (Eng.) (Appachan)

Mammy's eighth child, Appachan (K. T. Joseph B.Sc. Eng.), joined the Kerala State Government Service as an Engineer and now serves in the senior cadre of Executive Engineer. He married the eldest daughter of K. J. Mani of the Koottiparambil family in Vaikom. They have four children:

  • Mammikutty
  • Thomas
  • Kunjachan
  • Joji

9. K. T. Sebastian, B.A.

The ninth child, K. T. Sebastian B.A., looks after domestic affairs, estate management, and agriculture, residing at the ancestral Kathayanat homestead. He married Ethamma B.A. of the Kaniyamparambil family, Thathampally, Alappuzha. They are blessed with nine children:

  • Sanio, Marykutty, Jose, Rosy, Kuruvila, Mathukutty, Siby, Vincent, and Tessy.

10. Annamma

The tenth child, Annamma, was married in 1945 to Varkeyachan of the Illathuparambil family in Punnathura. They are blessed with seven children.

11. Claramma, B.A.

The eleventh child, Claramma B.A., was married to Ouseppachan of the Peediyekkal family in Athirampuzha. They have five children.

12. Rosakutty, B.A.

The twelfth child, Rosakutty B.A., was married in 1956 to K. V. Varghese, M.Sc., of the Kunthara family in Panavally. They were blessed with one son. Rosakutty departed this life at an early age on June 2, 1959.
